# Claims and reimbursements

When someone pays a company expense from their own card or UPI, a **Reimbursement Claim** tracks what the company owes them. Only confirmed bills paid by **Personal Card** or **Personal UPI** can be claimed, and a bill can be in only one claim.

## Each month

1. **Create the claims.** Open *Reimbursement Claim* and choose **Create Claims**. Pick any date in the month. Leave the category empty for one claim per person, or pick a category to claim just that category. Draft claims appear, one per payee.
2. **Check them.** Open a claim to see its bills, amounts and total. Remove a bill you do not want in this claim and it becomes claimable again.
3. **Choose the settlement.** *Reimburse* (default) means the company pays the person back. *Capital Contribution* means the person is leaving the money in the company, so approval records it as their contribution instead.
4. **Approve.** Submitting a claim approves it. Filter the list by month or payee, tick the claims, and use the list's bulk Submit. This needs the Accounts Manager role.
5. **Pay and record.** Pay the person outside the system. Back in the list, tick the approved claims you paid, choose **Mark Paid**, and enter the date and your payment reference.

## Seeing your own claims

A claimant whose login is linked to their Payee sees only their own claims. To get a voucher for a payee and period, use the **Reimbursement Voucher** report (month, date, vendor, invoice number, amount, category) or print the claim.

## Cancelling

Cancelling a claim frees its bills for a new claim. If the claim had been settled as a capital contribution, the contribution is cancelled with it.

## Why Create Claims found nothing

A bill is only picked up when it is confirmed, paid by Personal Card or Personal UPI, has a Paid By payee, is dated in the chosen month, and is not already in a claim.
