# Petty cash

Petty cash is company money taken out of the bank as cash. Each withdrawal is its own record, and the bills paid from it are checked against what is left.

## Recording a withdrawal

Open *Petty Cash Withdrawal* and choose New. Enter the date, the amount, the custodian (a Payee) and the bank reference. Spent and Balance are filled in by the system.

## Spending from it

On a bill, set **Payment Mode** to *Petty Cash* and pick the withdrawal. Paid By fills in from the custodian if you leave it empty. When the bill is confirmed:

- It must be no more than the balance left in that withdrawal.
- The withdrawal's Spent and Balance update. Cancelling the bill puts the money back.

Petty cash bills are company money, so they are never part of a reimbursement claim.

## Checking the balance

The dashboard shows the total petty cash balance. Open any withdrawal to see its own spent and balance, and the Bills list filtered by that withdrawal to see what was bought.
